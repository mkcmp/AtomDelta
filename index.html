What it does

From option flood to reviewable engineering decision.

AtomDelta turns human and AI-generated proposals into testable claims, margins, rejected branches, and reviewer actions before a team commits to an unproven hardware branch.

01

Change enters

A late requirement, component substitution, payload increase, or schedule-driven proposal crosses power, thermal, mass, qualification, or evidence boundaries.

02

Proposals become claims

Human, AI, vendor, or solver suggestions are normalized into explicit claims, affected margins, assumptions, evidence needs, and reviewer ownership.

03

Weak branches die

The margin ledger checks whether a tempting fix closes the governing constraint or merely patches a local symptom while leaving the real blocker open.

04

Humans review the packet

The output is a replayable packet: rejected path, conditional path, margin math, evidence gaps, reviewer questions, and approval boundaries.

Long-term direction

A modern Gym for engineering agents in the physical world.

Late hardware changes are the first bounded loop where the validation environment can be proven. The larger company is the validation layer for AI-generated physical engineering options.

Where proposed fixes become proof obligations

AtomDelta turns the option flood from AI systems, vendors, solvers, and humans into proof obligations, rejected branches, evidence gaps, and human-reviewable decisions.

  • Models propose branches.
  • Simulators, logs, CAE, and lab benches testify as witnesses.
  • AtomDelta records what survives review and why.
AI / human / vendor proposes branch -> AtomDelta converts it into claims -> witnesses provide simulator, log, CAE, or lab evidence -> margin ledger scores governing constraints -> false branches are rejected -> reviewer gaps stay open -> outcome memory compounds -> the next agent gets a better review environment
Boundary: AtomDelta does not approve hardware.

It produces decision-support packets for human reviewers. The current public proof uses synthetic and public-data cases; real customer-reviewed cases are the next required milestone.
